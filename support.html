Support

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Need help with TidyMinutes? Email everwiseprojects@gmail.com. The developer is Nikolaj Brandt, Denmark.

What to include

Describe what happened and what you expected. Include your app version/build, iPhone model and iOS version. A screenshot can help; hide personal information first. Never send passwords, API keys, receipts or card details.

Restore Pro

Use Restore purchases on the Pro screen with the Apple account used for the original purchase. An internet connection may be needed. This restores purchase access, not cleaning records.

Missing reminders

Check reminder settings, pause date and iOS notification permission for TidyMinutes. Focus modes and notification settings can affect presentation. Open the app to refresh its rolling schedule of up to 30 days. No reminders are sent on days without due or overdue tasks.

Cleaning records and deletion

Records are stored locally; there is no account or cloud sync. Deleting tasks or rooms retains completion history. Deleting the app through iOS Delete App removes its local data; offloading does not. Backups managed by Apple are separate. Both versions include manual JSON backup and validated restore in Settings.

Backup and restore

Open Settings → Backup & restore. Export your current data before restoring another file. Import a supported JSON backup, review the preview and confirm replacement. A PDF plan is not a data backup. Purchase data is never imported; reminders restore off and can be enabled separately. Keep your backup file in a safe location.

Print and PDF

Open Calendar → Print / PDF. Choose Calendar or Detailed checklist, a period, rooms and A4 or US Letter. Weekly plans are Free; month and year require Pro. Share PDF saves or sends a document; Print opens native printing. Overflow details may add pages. Browser preview has different capabilities from the installed iPhone app.

History and Quick clean

Pro history is in Settings → History, with month and room filters. Records without an old task-name snapshot are labelled rather than reconstructed. Quick clean is available from Rooms for 5/10/15-minute suggestions; tasks complete only when you mark them done.

Refunds and privacy

Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use. Email us for privacy requests.
