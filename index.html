FAQ

Simple on purpose.

Who is TinyMinutes for?

Project managers, consultants, team leads and anyone who runs recurring meetings and wants a clear record of decisions and follow-up without maintaining a complicated workspace.

Does everyone in the meeting need TinyMinutes?

No. TinyMinutes is designed to be useful to the person responsible for capturing and following up the meeting. The resulting record can then be shared with the people who need it.

Does TinyMinutes automatically transcribe meetings?

TinyMinutes is focused on structured meeting records, decisions and action items rather than trying to replace the meeting with an automated transcript.

Can I export or print meetings?

Sharing and professional export are part of the TinyMinutes workflow, with expanded export and print options planned for Pro.

When will TinyMinutes be available?

TinyMinutes is being prepared for iPhone. This page will link directly to the App Store when the public version is available.
