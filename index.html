FAQ

The essentials.

Does TidyMinutes make cleaning faster?

TidyMinutes is designed to reduce the planning and remembering around cleaning. It does not claim to make the physical cleaning itself faster.

Is TidyMinutes a timer?

No. The name reflects making progress in manageable amounts of time. TidyMinutes is a cleaning planner rather than simply a countdown timer.

Can I print my plan?

PDF and print options are part of the planned Pro experience, including week and month views.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. The planned Pro model is a one-time lifetime purchase.
