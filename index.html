Inside TidyMinutes

A routine that fits real life.

These features are implemented in the current development version. TidyMinutes is being tested before its public iPhone release.

01

Your rooms, your names

Start with eight room templates: Kitchen, Bathroom, Bedroom, Living room, Hallway, Office, Laundry room and Other. Add custom names, rename rooms and remove them with confirmation.

02

Tasks that fit your home

Choose optional suggestions for each room or write your own tasks. Edit names, estimated minutes and first due dates using a calendar picker.

03

Your own repeat rhythm

Repeat tasks every day, week or month, or choose an interval such as every two weeks. All repeat options are included in Free.

04

Look twelve months ahead

Browse a monthly calendar, tap a date to see its tasks and estimated total time, and return to today with one tap. Dates and week starts follow your device region.

05

See what needs attention

Overdue tasks appear together without a separate copy for every missed day. Future dates are projections and can shift when you complete or edit a task.

06

Done feels clear

Mark a task complete and see its green completion badge, last-completed date and next due date. Undo the latest completion if you tapped by mistake.

07

One gentle daily reminder

Choose a time or pause through a selected date. Receive at most one grouped local reminder per day when something is due or overdue. Permission is requested only when you enable reminders.

08

A little monthly encouragement

See completed tasks, rooms cared for and estimated minutes for the previous month. No scores or streaks. Dismiss the card for that month or switch Monthly summary off in Settings.

09

No account to set up

Rooms, tasks, history and preferences are saved locally and remain after reopening the app. Core planning works offline; Apple and RevenueCat handle purchase validation separately.

10

More room with Pro

Free includes two rooms, ten tasks, all repeat options, calendar, reminders and the previous month's summary. Lifetime Pro unlocks unlimited rooms and tasks plus older recorded monthly summaries.

11

Keep the progress you record

Completion events retain the room and estimated minutes from when you finished. Undo removes its linked event. Deleting a task or room preserves recorded history; older completions are never invented.

12

Your choices stay yours

Reminder time, pause, summary visibility and dismissed cards persist. Pro purchases can be restored through Apple. The current app has no advertising SDK.

13

Print a plan you can use

Choose a compact wall calendar or a detailed checklist, filter rooms and select A4 or US Letter. Weekly plans are free; Pro adds monthly and yearly plans. Busy calendars include extra detail pages instead of hiding tasks.

14

Back up your cleaning data

Export a manual JSON backup and restore it after validation, a preview and confirmation. Both versions include backup. Restoring replaces cleaning data; imported reminders stay off until you enable them.

15

Look back with Pro

Browse recorded completions by month and room, including deleted rooms. See task names where recorded, dates and estimated minutes, with totals for your filters. Earlier records without name snapshots are labelled honestly.

16

A quick clean, on your terms

Choose 5, 10 or 15 minutes and optional rooms. Get due and overdue task suggestions within your estimated budget, then tick off each task yourself. No countdown, score or automatic completion.

A note about reminders and records. Local reminders are scheduled up to 30 days ahead and refreshed when you use the app. They stop when that window expires without reopening. Monthly totals use recorded completions only; the first tracked month may be partial. Minutes are estimates, not measured cleaning time. Pro purchase restoration does not restore cleaning records.

FAQ

The essentials.

Does TidyMinutes make cleaning faster?

TidyMinutes is designed to reduce the planning and remembering around cleaning. It does not claim to make the physical cleaning itself faster.

Is TidyMinutes a timer?

No. The name reflects making progress in manageable amounts of time. TidyMinutes is a cleaning planner rather than simply a countdown timer.

Can I print my plan?

Yes. Choose a compact calendar or detailed checklist, selected rooms, and A4 or US Letter. Free includes weekly plans; Pro adds monthly and yearly plans. Share the PDF or print from the iPhone version. Busy calendars append detail pages.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase.

Can I back up my data?

Yes. Both Free and Pro can export a JSON data backup and restore a validated file. Restore replaces your cleaning records after confirmation and leaves reminders off. Keep your backup file somewhere safe; this is not automatic cloud sync. Restore purchases restores Pro access separately.

How does Quick clean work?

Choose 5, 10 or 15 minutes and optional rooms. TidyMinutes suggests due and overdue tasks using their estimated durations. If nothing fits, it offers the shortest eligible task with its actual estimate. You decide what to complete; there is no countdown or automatic completion.
