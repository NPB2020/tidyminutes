FAQ

Simple by design.

What is TidyMinutes?

TidyMinutes is an iPhone cleaning planner for organizing household cleaning into schedules, lists, reminders and calendar views.

Is TidyMinutes a timer?

The name reflects the idea of making progress in manageable amounts of time. TidyMinutes is a cleaning planner rather than simply a countdown timer.

Can I print my cleaning plan?

Print and PDF/export options are part of the planned TidyMinutes experience, including week and month views.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. The planned Pro model is a one-time lifetime purchase rather than a monthly subscription.

When is TidyMinutes available?

TidyMinutes is being prepared for iPhone. This page will link to the App Store when the public version is available.
