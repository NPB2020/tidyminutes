FAQ

Small app. Clear purpose.

Who is TinyMinutes for?

Project managers, consultants, small teams and anyone who runs recurring meetings and wants a clean record of decisions and follow-up.

Does my whole team need the app?

No. TinyMinutes is designed to be useful to the person responsible for capturing and following up the meeting, with outputs that can be shared with others.

Can I export or print my meetings?

Yes. Sharing and professional export are core parts of the TinyMinutes workflow, with expanded export options in Pro.
