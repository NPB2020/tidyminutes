Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

TidyMinutes is provided by Nikolaj Brandt, Denmark. For privacy questions or requests, contact everwiseprojects@gmail.com.

Your cleaning data

Rooms, task names, schedules, estimated durations, completion history and reminder preferences are stored locally in the app. We do not operate an account system or cloud synchronisation service for this data, and cleaning records are not sent to RevenueCat for purchase validation. Your device's backup settings may include app data in Apple-managed backups.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Apple processes in-app payments. RevenueCat helps validate purchases, restore access and manage Pro entitlements. This involves an app-user identifier (which may be generated anonymously), transaction and purchase history, entitlement status and technical information such as app/device platform, operating system and network information. We do not receive your payment-card details. Purchase-related services may communicate with their servers even when you have not bought Pro.

See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Reminders and monthly summaries

Reminders are scheduled locally with your permission. We do not register this app for a remote push service. You can disable reminders in the app or iOS Settings. Monthly summaries are calculated from locally stored completions; disabling their display does not delete history.

Advertising and analytics

The current app does not include an advertising SDK or a separate behavioral analytics service. Apple may provide platform diagnostics and TestFlight feedback under your Apple settings. This website uses no custom analytics scripts, advertising trackers or signup forms. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process visitor IP addresses and technical request logs; see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Support information

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to provide. Avoid including sensitive cleaning records or payment information. We use correspondence to answer requests and resolve problems, and retain it only as needed for support, dispute resolution and legal obligations.

Purposes, providers and international processing

We process purchase-related information to provide and restore purchased functionality, support information to respond to you, and necessary technical information for legitimate interests in operating a reliable service. Notification permission is optional and may be withdrawn. Apple, RevenueCat, GitHub and email providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States, subject to applicable safeguards and their terms. We do not sell personal information.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local records remain until removed from the device. Deleting a task or room retains its completion history. To remove all locally stored app data, delete the app using iOS Delete App (offloading is different); device backups must be managed separately. Purchase records held by Apple or RevenueCat are not erased by uninstalling and may be retained for entitlement verification or legal reasons. Manual in-app backup/export is not currently available; purchase restoration does not restore cleaning records.

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal information we hold, or object to processing where applicable, by emailing us. We cannot access cleaning records stored only on your device. You may complain to your local data protection authority, including Datatilsynet in Denmark.

Children and changes

TidyMinutes is a general household planner and is not directed at children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has supplied personal information to us. We will update this page when practices change, including before adding advertising or cloud features.
